KookaRide

Every Mackay bus timetable.

KookaRide is the simplest way to check bus and coach times in Mackay, Queensland. Timetables are built into the app, so it works even with no signal and no data.

  • Upcoming departures from stops near you
  • Search stops by name
  • Full route timetables
  • Trip planning across the Mackay network
  • Follow your trip from the Lock Screen
