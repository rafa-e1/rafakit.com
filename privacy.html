Last updated: October 21, 2026

RafaKit (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information in connection with our website, rafakit.com (the “Website”).

1. Scope

This policy applies to the Website and to communications you send us by email. Each RafaKit app has its own privacy policy, linked from the app’s App Store page and from our Apps page, describing the data that app collects and how it is used.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address, your name (if provided) and the contents of your message.
  • No accounts, cookies or analytics. The Website does not require an account, does not use cookies and does not use analytics or advertising tracking tools.
  • Hosting. The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect technical information such as visitors’ IP addresses for security and operational purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. How we use information

We use the information you send us only to respond to your inquiry, provide support, and keep a record of our correspondence.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not share it with third parties, except with service providers we use to operate our business (such as our email provider) or when required by law.

5. Retention

We keep email correspondence only as long as needed to respond to you and handle any follow-up, unless a longer period is required by law.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or ask us to stop processing it. To make a request, email self@rafakit.com with the subject line “Privacy request”.

7. Children

The Website is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Website.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last changed.
